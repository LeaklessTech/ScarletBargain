# Feedback Questionaire

## Quantitative Questions
The following questions are evaulated on the Likert scale (scale of 1 - 5, 1 being **Strongly Disagree**,  3 being **Neutral**, and 5 being **Strongly Agree**).

On a scale from 1 - 5, how much do you agree with the following statements:

**Question 1.1**
"I felt fearful of the monster chasing me." [1 - 5]

**Question 1.2**
"I felt it was important to save the prisoners in the simulation." [1 - 5 ]

**Question 1.3**
"I felt that the game was challenging." [1 - 5]

**Question 1.4**
"I felt that it was clear what I was supposed to do in the game." [1 - 5]

**Question 1.5**
"I felt that the gameplay was smooth and easy to control." [1 - 5]
