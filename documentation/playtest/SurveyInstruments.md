# Feedback Questionaire

## Quantitative Questions
The following questions are evaulated on the Likert scale (scale of 1 - 5, 1 being **Strongly Disagree**,  3 being **Neutral**, and 5 being **Strongly Agree**).

On a scale from 1 - 5, how much do you agree with the following statements:

**Question 1.1**
"I felt fearful of the monster chasing me." [1 - 5]

**Question 1.2**
"I felt it was important to save the prisoners in the simulation." [1 - 5 ]

**Question 1.3**
"I felt that the game was challenging." [1 - 5]

**Question 1.4**
"I felt that it was clear what I was supposed to do in the game." [1 - 5]

**Question 1.5**
"I felt that the gameplay was smooth and easy to control." [1 - 5]

**Question 1.6**
"I would like to play the game again." [1 - 5]

**Question 1.7**
"I felt like my decisions had an impact on the outcome of the game." [1 - 5]

**Question 1.8**
"I enjoyed playing the game." [1 - 5]

**Question 1.9**
"I felt immersed in the game world." [1 - 5]

## Qualitative Questions
A series of open-ended questions.

**Question 2.1**
What was your first impression?

**Question 2.2**
In your own words, what was the game objective?

**Question 2.3**
Did you find the game's premise exciting?  Why or why not?

**Question 2.4**
Did the controls make sense? How did they feel?

**Question 2.5**
Did anything feel clunky or awkward?  If so, what?

**Question 2.6**
Did you feel immersed?  If so, what contributed to that feeling?  If not, what pulled you out of the experience?

**Question 2.7**
Did the game feel too easy, too hard, or about right?

**Question 2.8**
If you could change one thing to make the game more fun, what would you change?
