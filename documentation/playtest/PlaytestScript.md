# Setup
Playtest conductors will setup a one-on-one playtesting environment in which they can see the playtester playing the game. This playtest can be conducted virtually (by setting up a screensharing and voice-sharing environment through an app like Discord) or in-person (by watching over the shoulder of the playtester).

Playtest conductors will ask a series of questions before and after the playtest to gather demographic information as well as experiential information relating to the game. The conductor will write down the answers either on pen/paper or in a digital environment such as Google Docs. In addition to written information the conductor may record audio of the playtest with consent of the playtester.

During the playtest the conductor will write down observational information relating to how the player is interacting with the game.

The time limit for each playtester is 8 minutes, though the playtester will be told they can continue playing after being notified of the time ending.
